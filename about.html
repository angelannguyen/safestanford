About Us

We are a coalition of students, faculty, staff, alumni, and campus residents opposed to mass surveillance at Stanford. We believe that automated license plate readers, facial recognition, and similar tracking technologies erode the trust, privacy, and academic freedom that a university community depends on. Mass surveillance chills speech, disproportionately targets marginalized students and activists, and treats every member of our community as a suspect.
